## Problem

Crowdfunding needs a transparent way to connect supporters, creators, and admins
around a shared, trackable economy.

## Approach

Creators launch campaigns, supporters contribute through a credit based economy,
and admins oversee platform activity, all secured through a custom JWT
authentication flow. Stripe in test mode handles credit purchases, and imgBB
hosts campaign images.

## Key Decisions

- Replaced the BetterAuth JWT plugin, which threw Base64 errors, with a custom
  `/api/token` route issuing HMAC signed JWTs via `jsonwebtoken`.
- Fixed an infinite redirect loop in the axios interceptor caused by improper
  handling of 401 and 403 responses.
- Designed a credit based economy that ties Stripe test mode payments directly
  to campaign contributions across three distinct user roles.

## Outcome

A role based crowdfunding platform running on a custom authentication layer
built to work around a library limitation.

## What's Next

- Campaign progress notifications for supporters who back a project.
- A public leaderboard of top funded campaigns.
- Withdrawal requests and payout tracking for creators.
